# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Thai-speaking casual players on mobile and desktop, playing together locally or practising against AI.

## Product Purpose
A Thai checkers board ready to play immediately. Support two local players and solo practice with clear legal moves and reliable rules.

## Brand Personality
Simple, composed, easy to play. Confirmed by the user during setup.

## Anti-references
No casino-style presentation, distracting effects, or marketing screen before play.

## Design Principles
The board is the main surface. Explain forced moves. Make state visible. Keep controls familiar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable Thai text, keyboard operability, visible focus, non-color state cues and reduced-motion support, as proposed in the confirmed direction.
